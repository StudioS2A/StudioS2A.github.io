  • Planning approvals

Process

From the first conversation to keys in hand.

  1. 01

    Consultation

    A site visit and a candid conversation about your brief, budget and timeline.

  2. 02

    Concept & Design

    Layouts, elevations and interior concepts developed and refined together with you.

  3. 03

    Documentation & Approvals

    Detailed drawings, material specifications and any statutory approvals.

  4. 04

    Construction

    Our own site team builds the project, supervised by the studio that designed it.

  5. 05

    Handover

    A final walkthrough, snag closure, and the keys — to a space that matches the drawing.

The Architect

Saksham Sharma, Founder & Principal Architect.

S²A stands for Saksham Sharma Architects. The studio carries his initials because every project it takes on is still led personally, end to end, by the architect whose name is on it.

His approach treats architecture, interiors and construction as one continuous discipline rather than three separate handoffs — an outlook shaped during his architectural education and carried into the studio's first projects across Delhi NCR.

"Good design shouldn't get lost between the drawing and the site. If I design it, I want to be the one making sure it gets built right."
