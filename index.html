The Studio

Most projects lose something between the drawing and the site. Studio S²A closes that gap — a design‑build practice founded by architect Saksham Sharma, offering turnkey solutions from the first drawing to the final handover. The person who draws the detail is the same person on site making sure it gets built that way.

We design for clients across India and build across Delhi and Noida. There is no fixed studio, by choice: the work keeps us travelling between sites, so we meet you where the project is.

The Architect

Saksham Sharma

Founder & Principal Architect

S²A, read two ways

S²A began as initials — Saksham Sharma Architects, two S's and an A. Read another way, it is the idea the studio works by: Structure to Architecture, taking every project from its bones to its finished form without handing it over halfway.

That outlook — architecture, interiors and construction as one continuous discipline rather than three separate handoffs — was shaped during his architectural education and is carried into every project the studio takes on. He works from wherever the work is, which is why the studio has no fixed address: time is better spent on site than behind a desk.

"Good design shouldn't get lost between the drawing and the site. If I design it, I want to be the one making sure it gets built right."
