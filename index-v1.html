The Studio

One studio.
Three disciplines.
Zero handoffs.

Most projects lose something in translation — between the architect's drawing, the interior designer's mood board, and the contractor's site reality. Studio S²A closes that gap by keeping design and construction under one roof, led by one architect, accountable for the outcome from concept to keys‑in‑hand.

Founded by Saksham Sharma in 2026 and based in New Delhi, the studio takes on architecture, interior design and full construction — independently or as a single design‑build engagement — for residential and commercial clients across the NCR.

How We Work

The Design‑Build Process

Five stages, one studio, no gaps between the people who design your project and the people who build it.

01

Consultation

We start with a site visit and a candid conversation about your brief, budget and timeline.

02

Concept & Design

Layouts, elevations and interior concepts are developed and refined together with you.

03

Documentation & Approvals

Detailed drawings, material specs and any statutory approvals are prepared and filed.

04

Construction & Execution

Our own site team builds the project, supervised by the studio that designed it.

05

Handover

A final walkthrough, snag closure, and the keys — to a space that matches the drawing.

The Architect

Saksham Sharma

Founder & Principal Architect

Studio S²A carries Saksham Sharma's own initials into its name — Saksham Sharma Architects, S²A for short — because every project the studio takes on is still led personally, end to end, by the architect whose name is on it.

His approach treats architecture, interiors and construction as one continuous discipline rather than three separate handoffs, an outlook shaped during his architectural education and carried into the studio's first built projects across Delhi NCR.

"Good design shouldn't get lost between the drawing and the site. If I design it, I want to be the one making sure it gets built right."
